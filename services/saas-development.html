SAAS ARCHITECTURE

What SaaS Solutions We Build

Complete commercial software foundations built for scale and monetization.

Minimum Viable Products (MVPs)

Fast, focused MVP engineering to validate your business model with paying customers before investing in large-scale infrastructure.

Multi-Tenant Architecture

Robust tenant isolation, row-level security (RLS), custom subdomains, and isolated workspaces ensuring zero cross-company data exposure.

Subscription Billing Engines

Automated recurring billing, seat-based pricing, tiered plans, free trials, coupon codes, and prorated plan upgrades via Stripe and Razorpay.

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Granular permissions, team invitations, audit log recording, single sign-on (SSO), and SAML integration for enterprise customers.

Superadmin Master Consoles

Backstage operational dashboards for platform operators to monitor MRR, churn rate, user signups, API usage quotas, and support requests.

Public APIs & Webhook Dispatchers

Developer ecosystems, API key provisioning, rate limiting, and webhook events that enable third-party tools to integrate with your SaaS.

PAIN POINTS ELIMINATED

SaaS Bottlenecks We Eliminate

Avoid the expensive architectural mistakes that cause 80% of SaaS products to stall.

Prolonged Time-to-Market

Spending 12+ months building before shipping. We use modular, battle-tested SaaS boilerplates to launch production-grade MVPs in 6 to 10 weeks.

Cross-Tenant Data Leaks

Improper multi-tenant design that leaks Tenant A's confidential data into Tenant B's session. We enforce strict database-level row isolation.

Complex Billing Edge Cases

Failed payment recovery, uncollected prorations, and manual invoice tracking. We engineer bulletproof webhook listeners for automated billing recovery.
