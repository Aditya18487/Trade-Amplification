FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions from Software Buyers

Key questions businesses ask when evaluating Corevix Technology as an engineering partner.

How does Corevix ensure our business owns the source code and IP? +
Ownership is clearly established in our master services agreement. 100% of all software repositories, architecture documentation, configuration files, and intellectual property rights belong to your company upon milestone delivery. We never hold your code hostage or charge recurring licensing fees for custom code.
Can Corevix integrate with our existing software, databases, and APIs? +
Yes. A major part of our engineering involves building secure RESTful or GraphQL APIs, webhook event handlers, and data sync bridges that connect custom modules directly into existing ERPs, CRMs, accounting systems, and payment gateways.
How do we track progress during the development process? +
We follow a transparent bi-weekly sprint cycle. You have access to working staging environments, weekly milestone demonstrations, clear task progress, and direct communication channels with senior engineers via Slack, WhatsApp, or scheduled video check-ins.
What happens after our software is launched? Do you provide post-launch support? +
Yes. Following live deployment and knowledge transfer, we provide comprehensive post-launch warranty support, ongoing infrastructure monitoring, feature enhancements, and dedicated maintenance agreements to scale with your business growth.
